One of the things our guests love most is that they never need the car to go for a swim. Here are the four beaches we send people to, in the order you reach them.

## Kitroplatia — 1 minute (100 m)

The closest one, practically at our door. A very nice, clean beach with coffee houses and traditional restaurants around it, so you'll have everything at hand: a morning swim, a coffee, lunch by the water.

## Ámmos — 10 minutes (650 m)

A small sandy beach with tranquil waters, and many places around to have a snack and a drink.

## Ammoudi — 18 minutes (1.2 km)

A large sandy beach with green waters. There are beach bars for a refreshing drink and restaurants with traditional cuisine for when you get hungry.

## Almiros — 26 minutes (2.1 km)

Worth the longer walk. Beautiful clear waters and golden sand, where the waters of the Almiros river meet the sea. There's a nice little bar, toilets and very friendly service.
