Agios Nikolaos is small enough that no area is really far from another, but each has its own rhythm. Sleeping next to the bars by the lake is not the same as sleeping on a quiet street above the sea, or staying a stone's throw from the harbour as opposed to in a hotel in Elounda. Here we go through the main areas with their advantages and drawbacks. We live in one of them, so we say so from the start and try to be fair to the others.

## Before choosing: three questions

- **Will you have a car?** If not, you want to be close to the centre, the harbour and the bus station. With a car, the outskirts and Elounda become much more convenient.
- **Do you want to go out at night or sleep without noise?** Nightlife is concentrated in a few streets; a few minutes away from them, the atmosphere changes a great deal.
- **What matters more: the beach, the views or having everything at hand?** The wider sandy beaches are at the far ends of the town; the centre has more services but less beach.

## The centre: Lake Voulismeni and the harbour

This is the heart of the town. Around [Lake Voulismeni](/blog/lago-voulismeni/) and on the streets that climb up from the harbour you will find cafés, restaurants, shops and bars, and from the harbour leave the boat trips around the bay and to Spinalonga.

![Dusk beside a small round pond ringed by rocky banks and tall trees, with cafe terraces, moored boats and people strolling](../../../assets/blog/gen/donde-alojarse-en-agios-nikolaos-1.jpg)

**Pros:** everything is a stroll away, you do not need a car and there is atmosphere at any hour.

**Cons:** it is the busiest area and, in summer, the noisiest at night, especially on the streets with bars. If you are a light sleeper, ask which way the room faces. We describe what the night has to offer in this area in [Agios Nikolaos at night](/blog/agios-nikolaos-de-noche/).

## Kitroplatia

A small bay with a beach, very close to the centre, surrounded by cafés and traditional tavernas. It is one of those areas where you swim in the morning, eat by the water and walk back to the lake in a few minutes.

**Pros:** beach and centre without needing transport.

**Cons:** the beach is small and tends to fill up in high season; at lunchtime and dinnertime, the promenade is quite busy.

## Mylos, by the sea

This is our area, so we know it well. The pension is on Sarolidi Street, in the Mylos area, by the sea and with open views over Mirabello Bay. From here, Kitroplatia beach is 1 minute away (100 m), the harbour 3 minutes (200 m), Lake Voulismeni 6 minutes (450 m) and the centre 9 minutes (600 m).

**Pros:** you are very close to the beach, the restaurants and the cafés, but without having them under your window. From the balconies facing the bay you can watch the sun rise over the sea.

**Cons:** if what you are looking for is a long sandy beach at the door, Ammoudi and Almirós are further away (18 and 26 minutes on foot). And if you like to stay out late, you will have a short walk back from the areas with bars.

## To the north: the Ammoudi coast

From the harbour, the avenue that runs along the sea to the north leads to Ammoudi, a beach in a sheltered bay about a kilometre from the centre. On this coast, on the way to Elounda, there are hotels and apartments facing the sea.

**Pros:** more peace and quiet than in the centre, the sea alongside and a pleasant walk into town.

**Cons:** the centre is no longer around the corner; to go back and forth several times a day, or at night, you may end up relying on taxis.

## To the south: Ámmos, the marina and Almirós

South of Kitroplatia, next to the marina, is Ámmos beach, with sunbeds and places to eat and drink around it. Further on, already on the outskirts, Almirós is one of the widest sandy beaches in the area, with a wetland where the river meets the sea.

![Wide sandy beach where a small river and a reed-filled wetland meet the sea, with tamarisks and hills in the distance](../../../assets/blog/gen/donde-alojarse-en-agios-nikolaos-3.jpg)

**Pros:** if beach days are your thing, staying near Almirós puts the sand within reach and gives you a more relaxed atmosphere.

**Cons:** the town is a long walk away, especially in the heat. With a car it is not a problem; without one, count on the odd taxi.

## Elounda

It is not a neighbourhood of Agios Nikolaos but a coastal village to the north, a little over 20 minutes away by car. Many of the big luxury hotels of eastern Crete are on its coast, and from its harbour frequent boats leave for Spinalonga in season.

**Pros:** peace and quiet, with the sea and Spinalonga very close. It is a good option if you prefer a resort-style hotel and want to spend a good part of your time in it.

**Cons:** to get to Agios Nikolaos, to Kritsa or on any excursion you depend on the bus, taxis or a car. You will find more about the area in [Elounda and Plaka](/blog/elounda-y-plaka/).

## Quick summary

- **To go out at night and have everything at hand:** the centre.
- **To walk to everything and sleep more peacefully:** Kitroplatia or Mylos.
- **For a wide sandy beach:** the Almirós area.
- **For a resort-style hotel:** Elounda or the coast heading north.
- **If you will not have a car:** the centre, Kitroplatia or Mylos. We explain it in [how to get around without a car](/blog/moverse-sin-coche/).

## Before booking

Whatever the area, look at the accommodation on a map and work out how many minutes' walk it is to the beach, the centre and the bus stop or car park. Also ask whether the room faces the street or the sea and how many flights of stairs there are to climb. If you would like to compare, on our [map with distances](/#location) you will find the distances from the pension to the main points of the town.
