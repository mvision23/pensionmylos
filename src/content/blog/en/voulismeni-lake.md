Leave the pension, walk for six minutes (about 450 m), and you're at the heart of Agios Nikolaos: Voulismeni Lake.

## Dinner by the water

The perimeter of the lake is lined with restaurants serving traditional Cretan cuisine. In the evening the lights reflect on the water and the boats line the quay.

## The view from above

Climb to the upper side of the lake for a stunning view of the town and the port.

## The legend

This is the famous lake whose bottom even Jacques Cousteau couldn't find. Stand at the edge and feel the connection of the earth and the water.

## Make an evening of it

From the lake it's a three-minute walk to the [port](/#location), with the statue of Europe's abduction, and nine minutes to the town center, with its coffee houses, shops and the beautiful orthodox church.
