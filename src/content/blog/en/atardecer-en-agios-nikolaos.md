Agios Nikolaos faces east, towards Mirabello Bay. That has a consequence worth knowing before you go looking for the sunset: here the sun doesn't set over the sea, but behind the town, beyond the hills and mountains to the west.

What you see from the town is something else, and in our opinion just as beautiful: golden light on the façades, the lake and the harbour changing colour and, on the far side of the bay, the mountains turning pink while the town is already in shadow.

## What the Sunset Is Like Here

In Agios Nikolaos the show isn't the disc of the sun but the light. There are three moments:

- **The last hour before sunset.** The low sun lights the houses from the side, turning them golden and then orange, and the water of the bay mirrors the sky.
- **The sunset itself.** You don't see the sun disappear, but you do see the light withdraw: first from the streets, then from the rooftops and, finally, from the summits on the other side of the bay, such as the Thripti massif.
- **The minutes afterwards.** The sky holds its colour for a while, the lights of the terraces come on and everything is reflected in the water.

![Whitewashed and ochre houses with blue shutters on a hillside glowing golden in low evening light, with a glimpse of sea](../../../assets/blog/gen/atardecer-en-agios-nikolaos-1.jpg)

The time varies a great deal with the season. At the end of June the sun sets at around 20:30, more or less, and in December shortly after 17:00. In summer it sets more to the northwest and in winter more to the southwest, but always behind land. The easiest thing is to check the exact time for the day in any weather app and head out half an hour early.

A word about the pension: our balconies face the bay, so what you see from them is the sunrise, with the sun coming up over the sea. For the sunset you have to move a little, though not far.

## In Town, on Foot

### The Viewpoint above Lake Voulismeni

The lake is about six minutes' walk from the pension (450 m). A cliff surrounds it on one side, and above there is a small park with a viewpoint, reached by steps from the shore. From there you can see the whole lake, the canal that links it to the harbour, the houses of the centre and the bay behind.

This is the place to see the town lit up in orange, as in the photo above, with the sun at your back. The steps are fairly steep: wear comfortable shoes and, if you stay until dark, use your phone torch to get down. We talk about the lake and its history in [our article on Lake Voulismeni](/blog/lago-voulismeni/).

### The Lakeside and the Bridge

Down on the path around the lake, the best moment comes just after sunset. The sky still has colour, the lights of the restaurants come on one by one and the still water doubles everything. The small bridge over the canal that connects the lake with the harbour is a good spot to watch it in both directions.

### The Harbour and Kitroplatia

The harbour is three minutes from the pension (200 m), next to the statue of the Rape of Europa, and Kitroplatia beach is one. From either of them you look east, with the bay in front of you. It's a comfortable option, with no steps: you sit by the water, watch the colour of the sea change and see the mountains on the other side keep the last light while the town is already in shadow.

## Outside Town

### From the Water

In season, boat trips leave the harbour to cruise the bay at sunset. From the boat the view is reversed: the town is in front of you and, behind, the sun is sinking behind the mountains. Companies, timetables and prices change from year to year, so ask at the harbour itself or check online.

### The Hills of Kritsa

Kritsa is a mountain village about 10 km away, on the slope that faces the bay. From the road up and from the village itself you see the bay from above, and in the late afternoon the light is lovely. The sun sets behind you, beyond the mountains, so here too you won't see the sun over the sea, but you will see the whole bay in the last light. It fits well at the end of an afternoon in the area; you'll find ideas in [Kritsa and the surrounding area](/blog/kritsa-y-alrededores/).

![Hillside with olive trees and dry-stone walls above a wide gulf in warm evening light, with a winding road and a village](../../../assets/blog/gen/atardecer-en-agios-nikolaos-3.jpg)

### The East Shore of the Bay

If what you want is the classic picture, with water in front and the sun going down in the background, you have to cross to the other side of the bay. The road towards Sitia climbs up the east shore and has several viewpoints. From there you look west, with the whole of Mirabello Bay in front of you and the sun hiding behind the mountains of the opposite shore.

It's a good way to end a car trip to the east (you'll find suggestions in [half-day driving routes](/blog/rutas-en-coche-de-medio-dia/)). Bear in mind that it's a winding road, with stretches above the sea, and that the drive back will already be in the dark: take it slowly.

## Practical Tips

- **Arrive in good time.** The best light is in the half hour before sunset and in the minutes after.
- **Check the time of day.** Between June and December the sunset moves earlier by more than three hours.
- **Bring a layer** in spring and autumn, especially if you go up into the hills: when the sun goes, it cools down quickly.
- **For photos**, the town looks best with the sun at your back. For silhouettes against the sky you have to look west, from the water or from the other shore.

After sunset, the centre comes alive. If you want ideas for the rest of the evening, you'll find them in [Agios Nikolaos at night](/blog/agios-nikolaos-de-noche/).

## To Finish

You don't need a car to watch the sunset here: the harbour is three minutes from the pension and the lake six, so you can walk there and be back for dinner. Choose the viewpoint to see the town gold from above, the lakeside for the lights and reflections, and the other shore of the bay if you're after the sun going down in the distance, over the water. And if one day you get up early, step out onto the balcony: the sunrise over the bay is worth it too.
