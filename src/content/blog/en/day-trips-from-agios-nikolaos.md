Agios Nikolaos is a very strategic place: clean seas, many beautiful beaches, and the ideal base for day trips across the whole Lassithi prefecture and beyond. These are the trips we recommend most.

## Spinalonga & Elounda — 23 minutes by car (15 km)

Discover the seaside village of Elounda, then walk amongst history on the island of Spinalonga. Wander the winding alleys and see the traces of the island's Venetian and Turkish past. Pass rustic houses and the ancient Venetian fortress, and learn about Spinalonga's past as the last leper colony in Europe.

## Chrissi Island, Ierapetra — 42 minutes by car (36 km)

A day trip to an island that will make you wonder if you are in Crete or the Caribbean.

## Cave of Zeus — 1 hour by car (45 km)

Explore the glorious Cave of Zeus and the unspoiled landmarks of East Crete. Visit authentic Cretan villages, connect with locals, and enjoy the amazing nature of the east of the island.

## Knossos Palace — 1 hour by car (65 km)

The world-famous archaeological site of Knossos and the iconic Museum of Heraklion: 3,500 years of civilization in one day.

---

Heraklion airport is 50 minutes away (62 km), so Knossos and the museum also fit nicely on your arrival or departure day.
