Agios Nikolaos is just the right size for a couple's getaway: small enough to explore on foot and without hurrying, and with enough variety around it (mountain villages, an island steeped in history, quiet beaches) to fill several days. Here are some ideas for planning them at your own pace.

## Start the day with the sunrise

From the balconies of the pension, in the Mylos area, you can watch the sun rise over Mirabello Bay. It is worth setting the alarm at least one morning: a coffee on the balcony, the light changing over the water and the mountains, and the town still quiet. In summer the sun comes up very early; if you are not early risers, spring and autumn make it easier.

Afterwards, Kitroplatia beach is a one-minute walk away (100 m). First thing in the morning there are few people and the sea is usually calm, so it is a good moment for the first swim of the day.

## Walks as evening falls

### Around the lake

Lake Voulismeni is six minutes on foot (450 m) away. At dusk the lights of the restaurants around it come on and are reflected in the water, among the moored boats. If you climb to the higher part, you will see the lake, the town and the harbour from above. There is a legend that the lake has no bottom; it is only that, a legend, but it is part of the charm of the place.

![Couple strolling from behind at dusk beside a small lake, with restaurant lights reflected in the dark green water](../../../assets/blog/gen/agios-nikolaos-en-pareja-1.jpg)

### From the harbour to Kitroplatia

The harbour is three minutes from the pension (200 m). That is where the statue of the abduction of Europa stands, recalling the myth in which Zeus, turned into a bull, carried Princess Europa to Crete. From the harbour you can carry on along the sea to Kitroplatia or head into the centre (9 minutes, 600 m), a short and pleasant stroll for after dinner.

If you would like to find a good spot to watch the sunset, we tell you about it in [sunset in Agios Nikolaos](/blog/atardecer-en-agios-nikolaos/).

## Dinners by the water

Between the lake, the harbour and Kitroplatia there are plenty of tavernas and restaurants with tables almost at the water's edge. We do not give names because places change from one season to the next, but here are a few guidelines that tend to work:

- Take a walk around before sitting down and notice where the local people are eating.
- Ask about the catch of the day and how they prepare it. Fresh fish is usually charged by weight, so it is a good idea to ask the price before ordering.
- Order several dishes to share: salads, seasonal vegetables, Cretan cheeses, something from the grill.
- In high season, if you want a table by the water, go early or book.

You will find more clues in [how to choose a taverna](/blog/como-elegir-una-taberna/).

## A day in Elounda, Plaka and Spinalonga

Elounda is about 23 minutes away by car (15 km). At the entrance to its small lagoon, some early 20th-century windmills have survived, and near the channel that connects it to the open sea there are remains of the ancient city of Olous, largely underwater. With a calm sea and a snorkelling mask you can make out some of the remains.

![Old stone windmills beside a shallow, glassy lagoon with a couple walking the shore and a rocky islet in the haze](../../../assets/blog/gen/agios-nikolaos-en-pareja-2.jpg)

A little further on is Plaka, a small village facing Spinalonga. From there the crossing to the island takes only a few minutes; from Elounda, a little longer. Spinalonga was a Venetian fortress and, between 1903 and 1957, a leper colony. Walking round it slowly, looking out to sea, is one of the most moving visits in the area. Bring water and a hat, because there is very little shade, and comfortable shoes for the stone paths.

To finish the day, there is nothing like having lunch or dinner by the sea in Plaka before heading back. You will find all the details in our guide to [Elounda and Plaka](/blog/elounda-y-plaka/).

## Quiet beaches

In high summer the town's beaches fill up by midday. To enjoy them in peace:

- **Choose your time well.** Early in the morning or late in the afternoon the sun is gentler and there are fewer people.
- **Walk a little further.** Almiros, 26 minutes on foot (2.1 km), is a sandy beach with shallow water where a small river reaches the sea, with a protected wetland right next to it.
- **Think about the season.** In June, September and a good part of October the sea is usually pleasant and there are far fewer people than in July and August.

Ámmos (10 minutes, 650 m) and Ammoudi (18 minutes, 1.2 km) are two other nearby options for a swim without a car.

## Kritsa and its surroundings

About 10 km from the town, on the mountainside, is Kritsa, one of the best-known traditional villages in eastern Crete. Its streets are full of little shops selling handmade textiles and embroidery, a tradition the village keeps alive.

![Weathered limestone walls and stone steps of an ancient hillside town, with a view of the bay between two hills](../../../assets/blog/gen/agios-nikolaos-en-pareja-3.jpg)

Shortly before you arrive stands the Byzantine church of Panagia Kera, with frescoes from the 13th and 14th centuries. And about 3 km from the village are the ruins of Lato, an ancient Doric city built between two hills, with views over the bay. It makes a good half-day outing, best in the morning or late in the afternoon, when the heat eases. We tell you more in [Kritsa and its surroundings](/blog/kritsa-y-alrededores/).

## Tips for the getaway

- **Do not try to see everything.** Two or three trips outside the town are enough for a week; the rest of the time, sea and strolls.
- **Get up early for excursions.** If you rent a car, setting off early lets you make the most of the best light and avoid the hottest hours.
- **Check the timetables.** The boats to Spinalonga, the archaeological sites and the museums change their hours with the season. Check them the day before, at the harbour or online.

A couple's getaway here works best without a tight schedule: the sunrise from the balcony, a swim before the heat arrives, an excursion or two and long dinners by the water. If you are unsure how to combine the plans, you can write to us at info@pensionmylos.com.
